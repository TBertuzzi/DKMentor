- Added **Gear Mentor** to DK Codex with Dashboard, Targets, Sources, Trinkets, and Upgrade Plan views.
- Added specialization-specific Season 2 gearing targets for Blood, Frost, and Unholy.
- Added live target ownership states: EQUIPPED / OWNED / TARGET.
- Added next-target guidance, loot-source grouping, crafting direction, Crest priority, Runeforge/enchant/socket snapshot, and current stat guidance.
- Enriched Builds with Hero Talent direction, focus, usage context, and review metadata.
- Added `/dkm gearmentor` / `/dkm gearing` direct access.
- Changed Rune HUD presentation to a Blizzard-like ordered pool: ready Runes left, spending visually from the right, recharge visually left-to-right.
- Gear/Rune changes are read-only/presentation-only; DK Mentor does not equip gear, spend currencies, change loadouts, or cast abilities.
- Preserved all 3.0.9 interrupt, Review, Coach, DK Tools, localization, and Midnight safety behavior.
