DK Mentor 3.1.2 is a focused live-test follow-up to 3.1.1.

## Fixed
- Preparation enchant cards no longer remain on CHECKING indefinitely when Midnight withholds a legacy equipped-item hyperlink. The checker now falls back to the native TooltipInfo permanent-enchantment line.
- Frost dual-wield Runeforge guidance is now shown as an explicit weapon pair: Main Hand first, Off Hand second. The main-hand recommendation follows the active Shattering Blade direction; Fallen Crusader is shown as the recommended off-hand rune.
- The Lich King commentary portrait now plays the model talking animation while commentary audio is active and returns to idle when the voice ends. The animation is refreshed while speaking because creature talking animations do not always loop by themselves.

## Preserved
- Read-only Preparation / Ready Check
- SBA-friendly build guidance
- DK Mentor layout export/import
- Movable/scalable/lockable Lich King portrait
- No bundled Blizzard audio or artwork

## Version
- DK Mentor: **3.1.2**
- Retail interface: **120100**
