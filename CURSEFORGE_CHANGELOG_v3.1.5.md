## DK Mentor 3.1.5

- Fixed the animated Lich King portrait continuing after the voice had already ended. DK Mentor now follows the real sound handle with `C_Sound.IsPlaying` and stops the animation/portrait when playback ends.
- Reduced the portrait shutdown interval for a much tighter audio/visual finish.
- Reduced the main DK Mentor window from 830x760 to 820x720 and compacted the Settings layout while keeping every existing control accessible.
- Preserved Arthas/Bolvar portraits, Layout Presets, Preparation / Ready Check, SBA-friendly guidance and Frost dual-wield Runeforge support.
