# DK Mentor 1.1.5

## DK Arcs polish

- DK Arcs can be moved again when HUDs are unlocked or Preview is enabled.
- The visible `Arcos do DK / Mover` header stays removed; dragging uses the invisible HUD area.
- Arc position is now saved independently from the Classic DK Resources position.
- Added **Arc opening** controls to move the two arcs closer to or farther from the character without resizing them.
- Fixed Runic Power orientation using dedicated right-side mirrored arc textures instead of runtime TexCoord flipping.
- Health still turns red at 30% or lower and the six Rune indicators remain centered below the character.
