# DK Mentor 1.3.1

## Empty aura bars now stay out of the way

- DK Buffs, External Buffs, and Debuffs automatically hide their empty background/title when no matching aura is active.
- The Blizzard Retail 12.1 AuraContainer itself remains enabled and shown internally, allowing new auras to appear immediately when Blizzard materializes them.
- HUD Preview and unlocked HUD mode intentionally keep empty aura frames visible so they can still be positioned.
- Compatibility/fallback aura bars continue to hide completely when their active list is empty.
- No SavedVariables migration is required.

This is a UI cleanliness update on top of the 1.3.0 DK Codex development line.
