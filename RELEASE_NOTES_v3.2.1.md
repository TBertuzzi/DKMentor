# DK Mentor 3.2.1 - Unholy Gear Data Hotfix

DK Mentor 3.2.1 is a focused data-maintenance release for World of Warcraft Retail 12.1 / Midnight Season 2.

## Unholy Gear Mentor update

- Updated the Unholy Best-in-Slot ring targets after the latest Wowhead gear refresh on September 8, 2026.
- Added **Vile Alchemist's Band** (Vashnik) as a current Unholy Finger target.
- Added **Sickening Signet of Atroxus** (Atroxus / Voidscar Arena) as the companion current Unholy Finger target.
- The obsolete **Band of the Amani Warlord** is not presented as a current Unholy BiS target.
- Updated Unholy Gear Mentor source metadata to **2026-09-08**.

## No other guidance changes

This hotfix does not change:

- Blood or Frost gear targets.
- Blood, Frost, or Unholy talent builds or Hero Talent recommendations.
- Unholy stat priority.
- Unholy trinket, crafting, Catalyst, or tier-set guidance.
- PvP guidance.
- Valeera Delve Mentor data or presets.
- Stats & Folio Advisor.
- Preparation / DK Ready.
- HUDs, layouts, presets, Survival Coach, or Lich King commentary.

## Compatibility and safety

- World of Warcraft Retail interface: **120100**.
- Addon version: **3.2.1**.
- Data version: **2026-09-08**.
- DK Mentor remains recommendation-only and does not automatically equip gear, change talents, alter Folio runes, configure Valeera, or perform protected combat actions.
