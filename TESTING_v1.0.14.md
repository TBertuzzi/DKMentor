# DK Mentor 1.0.14 test checklist

1. Enable **Bars only in combat**.
2. Attack a training dummy until DK buffs/procs and the ability bar are visible.
3. Stop attacking and wait for combat to end.
4. Confirm DK Buffs, External Buffs, Debuffs, Abilities and the interrupt icon disappear immediately after combat ends.
5. Start combat again and confirm the enabled bars return.
6. Repeat once in PvP and once after returning to World content.
