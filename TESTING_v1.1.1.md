# DK Mentor 1.1.1 live-client test checklist

## Visual validation

- Switch DK Resources to **Style: DK Arcs**.
- Confirm the old large square background is gone.
- Confirm the layout feels like a clean **side HUD**.
- Confirm Health bars appear on the left and Runic Power on the right.
- Confirm Rune mini-bars appear centered between the arcs.

## Editing behavior

- Unlock HUDs and verify the small drag handle appears.
- Lock HUDs and verify the drag handle disappears.
- Turn Preview HUDs ON and verify the drag handle appears again.

## Functional validation

- Test **Runes + Runic Power**, **Runes only**, and **Runic Power only**.
- Toggle power text ON/OFF.
- Confirm Restore DK Resources still resets the style and layout.
- Confirm there are no Lua errors entering/leaving combat.
