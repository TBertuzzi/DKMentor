# DK Mentor 1.3.1 test checklist

1. Log in with HUDs locked and Preview OFF.
2. With no tracked DK proc active, confirm the **DK Buffs** empty panel/title is not visible.
3. Trigger a tracked DK buff/proc and confirm the icon and bar chrome appear immediately.
4. Let the buff expire and confirm the empty panel disappears again.
5. Repeat with **External Buffs** by receiving/removing a helpful effect from another player or NPC.
6. Repeat with **Debuffs** by gaining/removing a harmful effect.
7. Unlock HUDs while an aura bar is empty and confirm its frame/title becomes visible and draggable.
8. Enable HUD Preview and confirm empty aura bars remain visible for positioning.
9. Lock HUDs / disable Preview again and confirm empty bars disappear.
10. Verify the Abilities bar and DK Resources behavior is unchanged.
11. Verify DK Codex, Loadouts, Dungeon Overrides, Loot Spec, Build HUD right-click, and specialization switching still work.
12. Reload the UI and repeat at least one empty -> active -> empty aura transition.
