2. Reload the UI and verify the resource-arc "Drag to move" handle is NOT visible.
3. Open DK Mentor settings and unlock combat HUDs.

6. Reload while HUDs had previously been unlocked; verify the new session starts locked and the handle stays hidden.
7. Enter combat while unlocked and verify the handle hides/disables while the arc HUD remains visible and click-through.
